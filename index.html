    Behind JF-1

    Your library already lives on your server. JF-1 is the pocket-sized way to take it with you.

    Made to be held

    Pick up. Tune in.

    One thumb on the wheel and your library starts to move. The display shows the cover, title and progress, and every key does what its icon says. A small tick answers each turn, and the Hold switch locks it all in your pocket. No screen to unlock and no app to open, so the song is playing before the moment has passed.

    Now playing

    Press play.

    Your whole Jellyfin library fits in a player that does one thing. There is no feed and no algorithm deciding what comes next, just your music. It plays on the train, in the mountains and on a plane, with no signal needed.
