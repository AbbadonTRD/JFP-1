Behind JF-1

Your library already lives on your server. JF-1 is the pocket-sized way to take it with you.

Made to be held

Pick up. Tune in.

One thumb on the wheel and your library starts to move. The display shows the cover and nothing else, and every key does exactly what its icon says. No screen to unlock and no app to open, so the song is playing before the moment has passed.
